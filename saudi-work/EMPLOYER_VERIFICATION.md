# Saudi Work Connect — Employer Verification Checklist

Use this checklist before any candidate profile is shared with an employer or any vacancy is described as verified.

## 1. Employer identity
- Legal/company name
- Saudi city / operating location
- Company website or official business presence, if available
- Responsible contact full name
- Responsible contact role/title
- Work email and phone/WhatsApp
- Evidence the contact is authorized to recruit for the company

## 2. Vacancy reality check
- Job title
- Number of workers required
- Saudi city / worksite location
- Monthly salary in SAR
- Working hours / schedule
- Experience requirements
- Skills / language requirements
- Accommodation: provided / allowance / not provided
- Transport: provided / allowance / not provided
- Expected start timing
- Confirmation the vacancy is genuine and currently authorized

## 3. Lawful employment process
Record who is responsible for the official employment/work-authorization process and confirm that the employer understands that candidate application through Saudi Work Connect is not a job, visa or work-permit guarantee.

Do not collect employer-side recruitment, work-permit or iqama costs from candidates. Final employment terms should be documented through the applicable official Saudi process.

Official references:
- HRSD Labor Law Article 40: https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/en/knowledge-centre/articles/64434-0
- HRSD contract-management service: https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/en/ministry-services/services/%D8%A5%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%82%D9%88%D8%AF

## 4. Candidate-sharing gate
Before sharing any candidate profile:
- Candidate is 18+
- Candidate consent to share with relevant participating employers is recorded
- No passport scan, national ID or bank details are included in the initial profile
- Only information relevant to the role is shared
- Employer identity and vacancy terms above are verified

## 5. Decision status
Use one status only:
- `NEW` — intake received, not checked
- `IDENTITY CHECK` — company/contact under review
- `ROLE CHECK` — vacancy terms under review
- `APPROVED` — employer + role verified for matching
- `REJECTED` — cannot verify / unsuitable / inconsistent
- `CLOSED` — role filled, withdrawn or expired

## First validation milestone
Do not add a large database, payments or public vacancy feed yet.

Proof target:
1. 1 verified Saudi employer requirement
2. 3 suitable consented adult candidates
3. 1 employer review/interview
4. outcome recorded without any job/visa guarantee
