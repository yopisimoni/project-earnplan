Questions before applying

Do you guarantee a job in Saudi Arabia?

No. Profiles may be matched with employer opportunities. Final selection belongs to the employer.

Do I upload my passport now?

No. Only provide passport status. Identity documents should not be sent through this first application form.

What happens if I match a role?

You may be contacted to confirm availability and proceed to employer review or interview.
