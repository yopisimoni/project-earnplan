Questions before applying

Do you guarantee a job in Saudi Arabia?

No. We help organize candidate profiles and match suitable people with available employer opportunities. Final selection belongs to the employer.

Do I need to upload my passport now?

No. For the first application we only ask about passport status. Sensitive identity documents should only be requested later when legitimately required for an official process.

What happens if my profile matches a role?

You may be contacted to confirm availability and information, and your profile may proceed to employer review or interview.
