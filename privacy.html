Pilot privacy notice

Your details are for the EarnPlan pilot.

What we collect

Name, email address, postcode/area, whether you are an earner or business, and information you choose to provide about a potential project.

Why we collect it

To run and validate the Bristol pilot, contact you about relevant pilot opportunities or requests, review potential matches, and understand whether the service is useful.

What we do not do

We do not sell your contact details. Joining the pilot does not guarantee work, income, a match, or acceptance of a business request.

Third-party processing

The pilot may use a form-delivery provider to securely forward submissions to the EarnPlan inbox. We will keep the infrastructure deliberately minimal while the concept is being validated.

Your choices

You can ask us to stop contacting you or delete pilot information associated with your email. A dedicated contact address will be published before public outreach begins.
