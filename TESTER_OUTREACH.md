# EarnPlan — First-10 Tester Outreach

Keep outreach small, relevant and manual. Goal: find UK students/early-career users who genuinely want extra income and can test the planner with real constraints.

## Priority contacts

1. University of Leeds — Joblink: joblink@luu.ac.uk
   - Student employment service for part-time, casual and temporary work.

2. University of Sheffield — Careers / Student Jobshop: careers@sheffield.ac.uk and studentjobshop@sheffield.ac.uk
   - Careers service and student job shop already serving students looking for work.

3. University of Hertfordshire — Careers: careers@herts.ac.uk
   - Careers and Employment service with part-time job support.

4. Royal Holloway Students' Union recruitment: SURecruitment@su.rhul.ac.uk
   - Student Union employment contact.

5. Regent's University London Student Union: studentunion@regents.ac.uk
   - Student Union contact.

## Outreach angle

Do not pitch EarnPlan as a job board or paid product. Ask whether they would be willing to share a free 90-second research test with a small number of students.

Suggested subject: Free student earning-planner test — looking for 10 UK testers

Suggested message:

Hello,

I'm testing a small free tool called EarnPlan that helps students turn an income target, available time and practical constraints into a ranked earning plan.

It is not a job board and does not promise income. The current test takes about 90 seconds and we're looking for only 10 UK users to tell us whether the recommendations are actually useful.

Would you be open to sharing the test with a few students who are currently looking for part-time or extra income?

Live test: https://yopisimoni.github.io/project-earnplan/

There is no signup required and testers can rate the result directly after completing the planner.

Thank you.

## Rules

- Send manually and individually.
- Do not send bulk email.
- Do not repeatedly follow up if there is no response.
- Never claim university endorsement.
- Never imply guaranteed earnings.
- Record only non-sensitive validation results.
